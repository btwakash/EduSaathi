About

EduSaathi brings the things a student needs into one place: planning, learning, attendance, doubts, community and career prep. It is designed to work on slow or unstable internet and to stay easy on the eyes.

How it works

  1. Today – add to-dos and deadlines, run Focus sessions, and let the app plan your day.
  2. Study – lectures you can download and resume, a virtual lab, notes summaries, video summaries, a Notebook and an AI chat.
  3. Plan – exam roadmap, attendance tracker (shows how many classes you can miss) and fair mock tests.
  4. Connect – class groups, co-working and study rooms, mood check-in, peer mentors, plus resume builder and mock interviews.

Good to know

  • Your progress saves automatically on this device, even offline.
  • Sign in to sync it across devices.
  • Make it yours in Me → Appearance: themes, accent colour, fonts, tap sound and motion.
