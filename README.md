## ✨ What can EduSaathi do?

| | Feature | What it does |

## 📲 How to install

1. Tap the **Download** button above (or open **[Releases](../../releases/latest)**).
2. Under **Assets**, tap **`EduSaathi.apk`** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file.
4. If Android asks, allow **"Install unknown apps"** for your browser or file manager, then tap **Install**.
5. Open **EduSaathi** and start studying! 🎉

> 🔄 **Updating?** If the update doesn't install, uninstall the old version first, then install the new one. Note that this clears the app's saved data.

## 🎬 How to watch a lecture

1. Go to **Study → Lectures**.
2. Paste a YouTube link, add a name if you like, and tap **Add**.
3. Tap **Resume** or **Play** to start.

**Player controls**
- **Tap the video** to pause or play.
- **Seek bar** at the bottom: drag it to jump to any part, or use **⏪10 / 10⏩** to skip.
- **Full screen** button: the video turns landscape. Controls hide automatically after 3 seconds. Tap the screen to bring them back (the video pauses), and tap **Exit** to come back.
- **Double-tap** the left or right side to skip 10 seconds.
- **Save & close** keeps your progress.

## 🤖 Using the AI Tutor

- Ask in your own language, like *"Ohm's law simple example ke saath samjhao"*.
- Ask follow-up questions. The AI remembers the conversation.
- For better answers, be specific: mention the subject, class or exam.

## ❓ FAQ

**The video says it can't play.**
Some video owners don't allow playing outside YouTube. Try another video. Also check your internet.

**The AI says "usage limit" or is slow.**
The AI has a per-minute limit. Wait about a minute and try again.

**The AI isn't answering.**
Check your internet connection and make sure you're on the latest version.

**Is my data safe?**
Your lectures, progress and notes are stored on your own device.

**Android says "App not installed".**
Uninstall any older EduSaathi version, then install again.
